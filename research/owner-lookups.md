# Owner lookups

Things that need a paid or OEM source. The owner looks them up in his own accounts (ALLDATA DIY,
Mitchell1 DIY, Motorcraft Service, ACDelco TDS, …) and drops notes or screenshots in
`research/owner-notes/` named `<year>-<make>-<model>-<engine>-<topic>.md` (or .png). We cite them as
"OEM service info (owner lookup, date)" or "ALLDATA DIY (owner lookup, date)", and use only facts —
never their wording, images or diagrams in our pages.
