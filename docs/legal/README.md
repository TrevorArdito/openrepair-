# Legal documents (DRAFTS)

The Terms of Service, Seller Agreement and Prohibited Items list live in
[`backend/app/legal/`](../../backend/app/legal/) so the app can show them at
`/legal/terms`, `/legal/seller-agreement` and `/legal/prohibited-items`.

**All three are drafts for lawyer review** (free option: a law school small-business clinic).
When a document changes, update its `Version:` line; sellers re-accept the Seller Agreement
when its version changes.
