# Business ideas (how ChrystalClear can grow and earn — without paywalling repair info)

Rule from CLAUDE.md: repair information stays free forever. Everything below either earns
money *around* the free core or is an optional paid business tool (Option B).

## Highest-value moves (recommended order)

1. **Get found on Google (biggest growth lever, costs $0).**
   People search "P0420 meaning", "2015 F-150 lug nut torque", "front brake labor time".
   Each code, guide and spec page should be its own Google-friendly page with a clear
   title and description. Today the app is a single-page app, which Google indexes poorly.
   → Add pre-rendered public pages (technical task, ~1 build step). This drives the traffic
   that makes ads and sponsorships worth something.
2. **Recruit 10–20 verified pro techs early.** They verify content, add labor times and
   trace wiring. Give them visible credit: profile badge, "top contributor" lists,
   reputation points. Community quality is the moat ALLDATA can't copy.
3. **Spanish early.** The app is already built for it. A large share of US technicians
   are Spanish speakers, and few repair tools serve them well. Cheap way to stand out.
4. **Labor-time data is a unique asset.** Real pro vs DIY vs book time, split by rust,
   from many techs — nobody publishes this openly. Promote it.

## Free-to-users revenue (allowed now)
- **Sponsorships:** tool brands, parts stores, local shops ("Supported by…"), clearly labeled.
- **Affiliate links:** tools in guide "Tools" lists and parts in part notes, via retailers'
  affiliate programs (check each program's rules; always disclose affiliate links).
- **Ads:** only after traffic exists; keep them out of the way of the shop workflow
  (never inside steps or specs). Privacy-respecting ad options preferred.
- **Donations:** GitHub Sponsors / Open Collective.
- **Job board:** shops pay a small fee to post technician jobs; techs browse free.
- **Hardware (later):** sell a recommended OBD-II adapter that works with the hardware module.

## Commerce modules (owner-requested, designed in docs/COMMERCE_DESIGN.md)
- **Affiliate parts links** — easiest first revenue; no inventory, no payments.
- **Parts marketplace (new + used, with fitment)** — revenue from a small seller fee per sale
  once payments are added. Fitment from our vehicle data is the selling point.
- **Tune store** — commission on emissions-legal tunes only.
- **State emissions info + catalytic converter section** — free content that drives search
  traffic and leads naturally to compliant parts listings.

## Optional paid business tools (Option B — later, separate modules)
- **Shop tools:** estimates/invoices using the community labor times, customer & vehicle
  history, technician assignment.
- **Digital vehicle inspections (DVI):** photo-based inspection reports texted to customers.
  Shops pay for these today; pairs naturally with our recalls/TSB data.
- **Team accounts:** shared garage, notes and job history for multi-tech shops.
- **Data/API access:** bulk access for other software (the free website stays free).
- **Training partners:** schools and ASE-prep programs (sponsorship or licensed classroom tools).

## Protect the project (cheap/free)
- Free trademark check through a USPTO law school clinic before spending on branding.
- Consider forming an LLC when money starts coming in (separates personal liability;
  a few hundred dollars or less in many states).
- Use a standard, lawyer-written contributor agreement (Apache ICLA style) before
  outside contributors join.
- Keep the "never copy competitors' content" rule strict — it's what makes the business safe.
