# River Valley Auto Sales and Market Seller Agreement

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. For review by a lawyer or legal clinic before launch.**
> Version: draft-2026-11-02

By listing items or accepting payments on River Valley Auto Sales and Market you agree to the
[Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md), the [Prohibited Items list](PROHIBITED_ITEMS.md) and this
agreement.

## 1. Your responsibilities
- You own what you sell or have the legal right to sell it. It is not stolen.
- Your listing is accurate: condition, fitment, title status, mileage, known problems and photos.
- **You are responsible for your own legal documentation and compliance with your state's laws**,
  including titles and bills of sale, odometer disclosure, dealer-licensing limits, catalytic
  converter and emissions rules, shipping rules for hazardous items (batteries, airbags, fuel
  parts), and any sales tax you owe.
- You ship within the time you promise and add tracking for shipped orders.

## 2. Fees
- Local pickup sales arranged through messages: **free**.
- Shipped / in-app sales: a **platform fee (currently 6%)** of the item price plus shipping,
  collected by Stripe as an application fee. **Stripe's payment processing fee is charged to you by
  Stripe.** The sell form shows the breakdown before you list. We'll give notice before changing fees.

## 3. Payments and payouts
- You receive payments through a **Stripe Express account**. Stripe verifies your identity and bank
  details, and provides tax forms (such as 1099-K) when required. You agree to Stripe's
  Connected Account Agreement.
- Money from each order is **held** and paid out after the buyer confirms receipt, or a set number
  of days after delivery, unless the buyer reports a problem.
- If a problem is reported, you can respond. We may refund the buyer or release payment to you.
  Card chargebacks are handled under Stripe's rules.

## 4. High-volume sellers (INFORM Consumers Act)
If you reach **200+ sales and $5,000+** in gross revenue in any 12-month period, you must provide
and verify your identity, bank account, tax ID and contact phone and email, and re-confirm them
yearly. Until then you can't post new listings or take in-app payments.

## 5. Enforcement
We may remove listings, hold payouts, or suspend selling when there is evidence of theft, fraud,
prohibited items or illegal activity, and we cooperate with law enforcement.
