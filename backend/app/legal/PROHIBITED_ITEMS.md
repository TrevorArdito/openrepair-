# Prohibited Items

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE. For review by a lawyer or legal clinic before launch.**
> Version: draft-2026-11-02

These can't be listed on River Valley Auto Sales and Market. Listings that break these rules are removed, and repeated
or serious violations lead to suspension and, where required, reports to law enforcement.

## Never allowed
- **Stolen goods**, or anything you don't have the legal right to sell (including items under a
  lien you can't release, or parts from a vehicle you don't own).
- **Emissions defeat devices and delete kits**: anything made or sold to remove, bypass or disable
  emissions equipment — EGR/DPF/DEF/SCR/catalytic-converter deletes, delete pipes and test pipes,
  O2 sensor simulators or spacers, defeat tunes, emulators. (US Clean Air Act.) "Off-road" or
  "race only" labels don't make these allowed.
- **Used or rebuilt airbags and SRS parts.** New airbags only from verified businesses.
- **Counterfeit or knock-off parts** sold as genuine/OEM.
- **Parts or vehicles with an open, unrepaired safety recall** that the seller knows about and
  hasn't disclosed and remedied where required.
- **Vehicles or parts with altered, removed or fake VINs**, or with odometers that were rolled back.
- Anything illegal to sell or ship in the seller's or buyer's state.

## Allowed only with conditions
- **Catalytic converters**: new ones must be EPA-compliant or CARB-approved (CARB needs an EO
  number); **used ones only from verified licensed sellers** with the documentation their state
  requires.
- **Whole vehicles**: the seller must have the title (or the state's legal ownership document) and
  disclose title status and liens; VIN required for 1981 and newer.
- **Hazardous items** (batteries, airbags, fuel components, refrigerant): ship only as the carrier
  and federal rules allow.
